# Content brief

## Steps
1. Confirm audience, goal (awareness, signup, sales), channel and length. Check USER.md for brand voice.
2. `web_search` what already ranks / trends for the topic; note gaps and angles competitors miss.
3. Produce the brief:
   - Working title (3 options) and one-sentence angle
   - Audience pain points (3)
   - Outline with H2/H3 and the key point of each section
   - Keywords / hashtags
   - Proof points with sources
   - CTA
4. Optionally draft the first version, then self-edit for clarity and length.
5. For recurring content, suggest a cron job (scheduled-report skill).
